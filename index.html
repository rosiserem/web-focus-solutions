About the Property

Mara Serena Safari Lodge

Masai Mara National Reserve, Kenya

5-Star Lodge Eco Certified Full Board

Offer Description

Experience the greatest wildlife spectacle on Earth at a 20% saving. The Great Migration sees over 1.5 million wildebeest and zebra thunder across the Masai Mara between July and October — and Mara Serena Safari Lodge sits in the heart of the action, perched on a hill overlooking the Mara River and the endless plains beyond.

This exclusive offer, negotiated directly by Kiboko Kenya Safaris, gives you access to the lodge's premium Full Board package at its lowest-ever migration-season price. Twice-daily game drives in open 4x4 vehicles led by expert Maasai guides, all three meals prepared from local ingredients, sundowner drinks on the viewing deck, and unlimited in-room wifi are all included — everything you need to immerse yourself fully in the Mara.

The lodge's 74 rooms are designed in traditional Maasai village style to blend seamlessly with the landscape. Each features a private balcony with unobstructed bush views. The solar-heated swimming pool, guided nature walks, cultural village visits, and optional hot-air balloon safaris (not included, bookable on site) make this one of the most complete safari experiences in Kenya.

What's Included & Excluded

Included

  • All meals (breakfast, lunch & dinner)
  • Twice-daily guided 4x4 game drives
  • Return airport / airstrip transfers
  • All park fees & conservancy levies
  • In-room wifi & laundry service
  • Evening bush talks & Maasai village visits
  • Swimming pool & fitness centre

Not Included

  • International flights to Nairobi
  • Kenya visa fees (~$50 per person)
  • Alcoholic & premium soft drinks
  • Hot-air balloon safari (optional, ~$480 pp)
  • Gratuities for guides & staff
  • Travel insurance (required)
