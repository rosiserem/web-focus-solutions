Let's Be Honest

Why Most Tourism Ad Accounts Quietly Fail

Tourism is not a normal Google Ads account. You've got booking seasons, long decision cycles, and the OTAs — Booking.com, Viator, SafariBookings — outbidding you on your own name. Most accounts fail before they ever had a chance. Usually for one of these four reasons.

1. Conversion tracking is broken

If Google can't see which clicks became enquiries, it optimises for cheap clicks instead of real leads. This is the number one killer — and most accounts I open have it wrong or missing entirely.

2. Wrong keyword match types

Broad match left on by default means you pay for "jobs in Maasai Mara" and "Mara weather in July." Your safari budget disappears on searches that will never book.

3. No negative keywords

Without a proper negative list, you're bidding against free, cheap, jobs, salary, map, distance — the searches that drain budget and never convert. In tourism this list is long and specific.

4. Budget spread too thin

Ten half-funded campaigns instead of a few focused ones. During peak booking season you need the budget concentrated where travelers are ready to book — not scattered.
